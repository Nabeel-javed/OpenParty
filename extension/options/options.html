OpenParty settings

You

Sync server

Parties are relayed through this server. Use the default, or run your own (see the README). Invite links carry the server, so friends join the right one automatically.

Site access

OpenParty asks once for access to websites, the first time you start or join a party. It never runs on a page unless that tab is in a party.

Privacy

OpenParty has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. The sync server only sees what a party needs (video link and title, playback position, names, chat) and forgets a party 10 minutes after everyone leaves.
