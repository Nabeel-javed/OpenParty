OpenParty lets you watch videos together with friends, wherever they are. Start a party on the video you're watching, send the invite link, and everyone's playback stays in sync while you chat and react.

It's free and open source. No accounts, no premium tier, no ads and no tracking.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open a video and click the OpenParty icon.
2. Press "Start a party" and copy the invite link.
3. Friends open the link and land right in the party, on the same video, at the same moment.

FEATURES
• Works on most websites with a video player, including players embedded in other pages.
• Anyone can play, pause or seek, and everyone follows. The host can lock control so only they can.
• Chat, emoji reactions and a member list in a panel next to the video, also in fullscreen.
• When the host moves to the next episode or video, everyone goes along.
• Handles ads, buffering and live streams: anyone who falls behind catches up automatically.
• Pick a name and avatar. No sign-up.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• OpenParty only runs in tabs that are in a party.
• It collects nothing: no analytics, no trackers, no ads.
• The sync server only relays what a party needs (the video link and title, playback position, names and chat) and deletes a party 10 minutes after everyone leaves.
• Want full control? Run your own server for free and point OpenParty at it.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Everyone in the party needs their own access to the site being watched (their own subscription for paid services). OpenParty doesn't stream or share video; it keeps each person's own player in sync.
• The first time you start or join a party, OpenParty asks once for access to websites, so it can find and sync the video on any site.
